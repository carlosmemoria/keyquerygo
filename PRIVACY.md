# Privacy policy

KeyQueryGo does not collect analytics, browsing history, keyboard logs or user identifiers.
Destination settings remain in chrome.storage.local on the user's device.
Keyboard events are processed briefly in memory to recognize sequences.
Only a submitted search term is sent directly to the selected destination.
Search providers apply their own privacy policies.
Exported settings include URLs; inspect them for private information before sharing.
